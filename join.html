Join & contact

Come as you are

Cresco Nexus is for any student who wants to learn, explore, build, connect, and grow — no specific technology, department, year, or expertise required. The primary audience is BCA and BSc Computer Science students; the community is open to all departments.

Who can join

No prerequisites. Ever.

Join as a complete beginner or with existing experience. There is no entrance test, no prior knowledge requirement, and no fixed track.

Students progress at their own pace: Beginner → Learner → Contributor → Speaker → Mentor as skills develop.

All departments are welcome. Computer Science students focus on current technologies and practical skills; BBA, BCom, and BA English students bring business and communication strengths into cross-department projects.
