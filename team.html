The people behind the community

Guided by faculty, run by students

Cresco Nexus is student-led with faculty supervision. Students plan, coordinate, and run day-to-day activities; the Faculty Coordinator provides institutional oversight, guidance, and support.

Everyone else

General members are the community

Beyond the core team, every member participates as learners, contributors, speakers, project members, volunteers, organizers — and mentors, as skills develop.

The core team keeps evolving too. Experienced members gradually take responsibility for sessions, projects, mentoring, documentation, and coordination, so the community remains sustainable beyond any single team.

How decisions get made
The Student Lead and core team coordinate day-to-day operations, keeping faculty informed. Major decisions, external engagements, and institutional approvals go through college channels. Records are maintained for review and continuity.

This is your community too

Core team roles rotate and grow with the community. You don't have to start at the front — every mentor was once a beginner.
