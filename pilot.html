Pilot plan

Three months to discover, learn, build & share

Cresco Nexus launches as a three-month pilot using existing college resources, under faculty supervision with a student core team. It's a testing and learning phase — the pilot is where the community finds out what students actually need.

How the pilot works

A simple, sustainable rhythm

Each month holds two core activities — a learning session and an interactive activity — supported by the One Skill. One Month. challenge. Everything else adapts around it.

  • Before it begins

    Announce Cresco Nexus, open membership, collect interests and skill levels, find initial student contributors, and confirm facilities and approvals.

  • During each month

    Run the learning session and activity, support One Skill progress, encourage peer participation, and document student outputs.

  • At each month's end

    Collect brief feedback, review participation and outputs, recognize meaningful contributions, and adjust the next month's plan.

The roadmap

Month by month

Discover → Learn → Build & Share. Each month builds on the one before it.

Month 1

Discover

Help students explore skills, fields, and opportunities.

Learning session

“What Can You Do With Your Degree?” — fields, technologies, career directions, and practical opportunities.

Interactive activity

Technology / career exploration quiz or team challenge.

One Skill

Each student selects one skill to develop and creates a learning plan.

Outcome: Students understand Cresco's purpose, explore options, and identify an interest area. Working output includes a member-interest map and initial One Skill plans.

Month 2

Learn

Help students learn independently and find good resources.

Learning session

“How to Find Good Resources and Build a Learning Roadmap.”

Interactive activity

Resource-hunt or roadmap-building challenge.

One Skill

Continue the previous skill, or choose a new one based on interest.

Outcome: Students develop independent learning skills rather than relying on classroom instruction — individual roadmaps and visible One Skill progress.

Month 3

Build & Share

Apply learning and demonstrate progress.

Learning session

“From Learning to Practical Project.”

Interactive activity

Mini-project challenge or student showcase.

One Skill

Demonstrate progress — what was accomplished, what was difficult, what's next.

Outcome: Students experience the full progression — learn → practice → apply → share — with practical outputs and showcase material.

The rhythm

A typical month inside Cresco Nexus

Every month follows the same operating cycle, so members always know what's expected. Exact dates flex around the academic calendar and exams.

1

2

3

Run the interactive activity, challenge, project task, or peer-learning exercise.

4

Month by month, week by week

What actually happens

The detail behind the three months — taken straight from the pilot execution plan.

1 Month 1 — Discover

  • Week 1

    Orientation, introduction to Cresco Nexus, membership registration, and collection of student interests.

  • Week 2

    Learning session — “What Can You Do With Your Degree?” with examples of fields, technologies, and career directions.

  • Week 3

    Technology/career exploration quiz or team challenge; identify the areas with strongest student interest.

  • Week 4

    Students select their One Skill goal and create a simple learning plan with a starting resource and expected outcome.

2 Month 2 — Learn

  • Week 1

    Review One Skill progress and common learning difficulties.

  • Week 2

    Learning session — “How to Find Good Resources and Build a Learning Roadmap.”

  • Week 3

    Resource-hunt or roadmap-building challenge; practice evaluating resources and turning goals into smaller tasks.

  • Week 4

    Peer discussion and progress review; students continue learning and practising their selected skill.

3 Month 3 — Build & Share

  • Week 1

    Introduce the transition from learning material to practical output; help students define a small achievable project.

  • Week 2

    Project-building period with peer support and optional mentor guidance.

  • Week 3

    Mini-project challenge or preparation for the student showcase.

  • Week 4

    Showcase and reflection — what was attempted, resources used, what was built, difficulties faced, what they can now do, and what they plan to learn next.

End of the pilot

What happens after three months

The core team prepares a short review for the Faculty Coordinator covering participation, activities conducted, One Skill progress, student outputs, feedback, challenges, and resource requirements.

Results shape the next phase — what to continue, modify, or introduce, and what support the college may need to provide.
